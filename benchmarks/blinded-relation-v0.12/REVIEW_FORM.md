# V0.12 Review Form

Copy one block per assigned case.

```text
challenge_id:
gold_treatment:
confidence:
evidence_span:
notes:
reviewer_role:
```

Allowed `gold_treatment` values:

```text
AFFIRMATIVE_USE
DISTINGUISHES_OR_LIMITS
NEGATIVE_TREATMENT
MENTION_ONLY
UNKNOWN
```

Allowed `confidence` values:

```text
low
medium
high
```

Please do not open `predictions.jsonl` before submitting your review.
