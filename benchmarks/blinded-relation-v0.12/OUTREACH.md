## Legal Hackers / LinkedIn version

I'm building an open-source LegalTech reliability tool called Legal Authority
Diff. It tests whether an AI-system change weakens the legal authority supporting
a claim.

We've reached a stage where the engineering bottleneck is no longer code but
ground-truth quality. I have already frozen predictions for a new 20-case U.S.
Supreme Court benchmark *before* creating gold labels.

I'm looking for one or two independent reviewers who have **not** seen those
predictions. The review is small: 5–10 short passages, each labeled as one of:

- applies / relies on / reaffirms precedent;
- distinguishes / limits precedent;
- negative treatment;
- mention only;
- unknown.

This is research annotation only, not legal advice or work on a live matter.
The passages come from official U.S. Reports / GovInfo sources.

A 5-case packet should take around 15–30 minutes. Credit can be given publicly
in the repository if desired.

Project:
https://github.com/othy19904-eng/legal-authority-diff

If this sounds interesting, I can send the blind packet without the model's
